# 1. Doctrine audit

## D1 — Marketing can change the business

**PASS.**

The chapter presents marketing as part of business-system design and explicitly allows target, offer, price, process, sales method, experience and capacity assumptions to be challenged when they prevent sustainable value creation.

It does not reduce marketing to promotion.

## D2 — Promotion amplifies what exists

**PASS.**

`LineaCasa` demonstrates that additional demand can amplify existing customer-mix, quoting and delivery constraints.

The chapter does not convert this into the reverse absolute that promotion is never the problem.

## D3 — System feedback

**PASS.**

Figure 1.1 and the surrounding prose show that sales, delivery, economics and capacity create feedback toward earlier decisions.

The model is not taught as an irreversible funnel.

## D4 — Strategy before tools

**PASS.**

The automation/AI comparison demonstrates that the same tool produces different value depending on whether the underlying commercial states, decisions and ownership are understood.

Execution may be delegated; judgment criteria remain a management responsibility.

## D5 — Cause, amplifier and symptom

**PASS.**

The chapter distinguishes the three roles and uses both `LineaCasa` and the local landing-page failure counterexample to prevent simplistic upstream blame.

## D6 — No obsolete tool/AI absolute

**PASS.**

The chapter does not claim that tools cannot outperform users or other historical absolutes. The active principle is preserved: tools can accelerate execution while management remains responsible for direction, criteria and quality control.

- P1: 0
- P2: 0 blocking

# 2. Instructional audit

## Opening tension

**PASS.**

The reader first sees a plausible local win: more qualified inquiries, more contracts and more revenue.

The downstream deterioration is revealed progressively rather than announced as a moral in the first paragraph.

This creates a real learning problem instead of beginning with a definition.

## Beginner path

**PASS after revision.**

Early drafts introduced `CAC`, contribution terminology and specialist vocabulary too quickly. The revised chapter explains the economic meaning in ordinary Italian before Chapter 2 formalizes the terminology.

Figure 1.1 also replaces premature `posizionamento`/`referral` labels with `ragione di scelta` and `passaparola`.

## Worked reasoning

**PASS.**

The chapter does not merely state that local metrics can mislead. It walks through why the LineaCasa interpretation changes when acquisition cost, job economics, quote delay and supervisory capacity are added.

## Counterexample

**PASS.**

The broken mobile form example shows that system thinking can rationally conclude that the first fix is local and tactical when evidence localizes the failure.

This prevents a new ideology in which every symptom must hide a deep strategic cause.

## Transfer

**PASS.**

The software-partnership scenario asks the reader to distinguish observed facts from hypotheses and to identify evidence that would discriminate competing explanations.

It does not provide a falsely certain diagnosis from intentionally incomplete evidence.

## Bridge to next chapter

**PASS.**

The chapter ends because the system model creates a new unresolved need: the reader must understand contribution, acquisition cost, cash timing and capacity well enough to judge system outcomes.

Chapter 2 therefore follows causally rather than editorially.

### Instructional finding

- P0: 0
- P1: 0
- P2: 0 blocking

# 3. Editorial / AI-smell audit

## List dependence

**PASS.**

The reader-facing chapter is carried by prose, case, table, figures and one principle box.

There is no bullet cascade explaining the commercial system.

## Paragraph rhythm

**PASS.**

Paragraphs generally develop a thought across several connected sentences. Short paragraphs appear as actual turns in the argument rather than as default chat rhythm.

## Repeated negation

**PASS after revision.**

The initial `Non ancora...` sequence was removed. The chapter uses contrast where required but does not build its voice from serial `not X but Y` constructions.

## Repeated rhetorical questions

**PASS after revision.**

The initial cluster of similarly shaped diagnostic questions was replaced by discursive authorial reasoning.

## Authorial presence

**PASS.**

The prose makes judgments visible: which metric is insufficient, why four plausible interventions are not yet a diagnosis, what evidence would differentiate hypotheses and when a local fix is justified.

It does not rely on autobiography or persona imitation.

## Heading density

**PASS.**

Nine PDF pages contain only major argumentative section changes. Headings do not function as a substitute for paragraph transitions.

## Systematic V1 smell

**NOT OBSERVED at blocking level.**

The rendered pages do not exhibit the V1 pattern of `heading → bullet cascade → isolated punchline → new heading`.

# 5. Depth decision

The draft is approximately **3,367 words**, below the provisional 4,500–7,000 band stated in `SPEC.md`.

Decision:

**DO NOT PAD.**

The lower word count is acceptable for the prototype because all required learning movements are present:

- concrete opener;
- system model;
- cause/amplifier/symptom distinction;
- tool/strategy contrast;
- tactical counterexample;
- competing-hypothesis walkthrough;
- transfer case;
- causal bridge to economics.

If founder review reveals that the chapter feels rushed, the correct response is to identify the missing reasoning/example, not to add generic prose to reach a target count.

This is evidence that V2 chapter bands should remain chapter-specific rather than being treated as a uniform book-wide length target.

# 6. System lessons from Golden A

Golden A validates several V2 design decisions:

1. recurring synthetic cases can carry real conceptual depth if facts/numbers are locked first;
2. ordinary-language pre-training should precede professional terminology;
3. figures should be drafted during the argument, not appended after the chapter is written;
4. a counterexample is essential when a strong principle risks becoming a new absolute;
5. one earned box is more useful than repeated callouts;
6. the word-count range is a diagnostic constraint, not a production quota;
7. actual PDF inspection exposes learning/design issues that Markdown review does not.

No change to Layer 1 doctrine is required.

No major change to the 31-chapter architecture is required by Golden A.
