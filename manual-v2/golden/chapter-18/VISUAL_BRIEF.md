# Golden C — Visual Brief

## Chapter

V2 Chapter 18 — Consultative selling: diagnosis before prescription

Working reader-facing title: **Vendere senza prescrivere al buio**

## Visual objective

The visuals must make visible three things that prose alone tends to blur:

1. a consultative sale is a sequence of decision jobs with branches, not a straight script;
2. different people can hold different kinds of decision power;
3. a salesperson should separate known facts from interpretations and missing evidence.

No funnel graphics. No “7 steps to close” poster.

---

# Figure 18.1 — Diagnostic sales process with branches

## Teaching job

Show the stable spine of the process without implying every conversation uses identical wording or always ends in a proposal.

## Preferred structure

Main path:

`transferred context → diagnose current situation → deepen criteria/consequences → map stakeholders/timing → summarize and verify diagnosis → prescribe if fit → proof against specific uncertainty → proposal/next decision`

Branches:

- insufficient evidence → request artifact/data → return to diagnosis;
- missing stakeholder → schedule joint validation → return;
- no fit → no sale/referral/stop;
- vague objection → identify missing confidence/information → return to relevant node;
- clear fit + sufficient confidence → decision.

## Visual semantics

- main spine = solid lines;
- diagnostic returns = dashed arrows;
- no-sale branch clearly legitimate, not shown as failure/red warning;
- use short labels;
- avoid numbered “closing steps”.

## Reader takeaway

> The process is standardized by the jobs it must accomplish; the conversation remains adaptive.

# Figure 18.2 — Stakeholder decision map

## Teaching job

Show that `decision maker` is often too crude a label.

For Ferroline Servizi:

- Laura/CFO — sponsor + economic authority for normal €6k project;
- Marco/Sales Director — owns pipeline process/definitions and user adoption;
- Elisa/IT Manager — technical access/security veto;
- CEO/board — consumes final management information and sets reliability expectation.

## Preferred form

Central decision: `reliable management reporting before board cycle`.

Around it, four nodes connected by different labeled roles:

- economic authority;
- process ownership;
- technical veto/enablement;
- final use/governance.

Do not render as hierarchy/org chart.

## Reader takeaway

A salesperson can have budget approval and still lack the process/technical participation required to deliver the promised result.

# Artifact 18.1 — Seller diagnostic notes

## Teaching job

Make reasoning auditable.

Show a compact four-column working note after the first diagnostic conversation:

| FACT | HYPOTHESIS | OPEN / TO VERIFY | NEXT EVIDENCE |
|---|---|---|---|
| 18 h/week manual reconciliation reported by CFO | dashboard problem may be downstream of inconsistent definitions | are the 18 hours typical across months? | sample last 2 reporting cycles |
| pipeline defined differently across branches | one unified number may mix incomparable states | which definitions can Sales accept? | joint session CFO + Sales |
| spreadsheet adjustments exist | source-of-truth ownership unclear | which adjustments affect board metrics? | branch files + IT source map |
| board in ~5 weeks | timing pressure drives four-week request | what minimum view is actually needed for board? | confirm board metric set |

The artifact should explicitly distinguish fact supplied by the prospect from seller inference.

## Form

Could be a table in prose or an SVG/annotated notebook style. For Golden C prototype prefer an SVG/table-like exhibit to test artifact integration.

# Optional micro-visual — three confidence objects

Only add if the objection section is unclear without it.

Could show:

- confidence in proposed solution;
- confidence in professional/seller;
- confidence in company/delivery.

Do not imply numeric scoring or that all objections map perfectly to one box.

---

# Dialogue annotation

Do not place full dialogue in speech bubbles.

If annotation is useful, use short margin-like labels in prose such as:

- `job: clarify result`
- `job: deepen consequence`
- `job: test stakeholder gap`

But avoid turning every line into instructional markup.
