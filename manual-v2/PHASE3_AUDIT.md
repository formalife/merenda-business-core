Date: 2026-09-20

# Gate 1 — Failure modes are evidence-based

**PASS.**

The style critique is tied to direct V1 evidence rather than generic complaints about AI writing.

Measured/observed signals include:

- high list density;
- hundreds of bullet runs;
- repeated short negation-led sentences;
- recurrent metadiscursive openings;
- telegraphic paragraph rhythm;
- heading/list architecture that survives into PDF despite professional typography.

The catalog explicitly avoids treating any one feature as proof of machine authorship.

---

# Gate 2 — The system defines a positive voice

**PASS.**

The Style Bible defines the target as an experienced operator-teacher and specifies:

- visible expert judgment;
- paragraph development;
- sentence-rhythm variation;
- list/heading discipline;
- problem-led terminology;
- contextual qualification;
- adult-to-adult tone;
- explanation-first quantitative writing;
- narrative continuity.

The system is therefore not dependent on a blacklist of phrases.

---

# Gate 3 — Rhetorical diversity is operationalized

**PASS.**

V2 drafting is explicitly allowed to alternate among:

- scene;
- analysis;
- definition;
- worked example;
- counterexample;
- figure;
- calculation;
- case;
- procedure;
- synthesis.

Consistency is required at the level of learning quality, not repeated section choreography.

---

# Gate 4 — Lists/headings/short paragraphs are controlled, not banned

**PASS.**

The guidance avoids a simplistic reverse-template.

It permits:

- checklists;
- procedures;
- reference sets;
- short rhetorical emphasis;
- meaningful structural headings.

The decision rule is functional rather than numerical.

---

# Gate 5 — V1→V2 transformations demonstrate the change

**PASS.**

Ten transformations cover different failure modes, including:

- bullets/list logic → causal prose;
- telegraphic sentences → integrated argument;
- abstract rule → scene;
- definition cascade → problem-led terminology;
- repeated negation → positive model;
- checklist-first → explanation-first;
- rhetorical-question rhythm → visible authorial reasoning;
- sterile arithmetic → worked business case;
- generic caveat → contextual boundary;
- synthetic punchline → earned synthesis.

These examples are direction-of-travel demonstrations, not locked final prose.

---

# Gate 6 — Research coherence

**PASS.**

The style diagnosis is consistent with current research that finds more repeated syntactic/discourse templates and less structural variability in model-generated text than human-reference text.

The project does not optimize for AI-detector scores and explicitly rejects deliberate randomness/error insertion as a goal.

---

# Gate 7 — No doctrine contamination

**PASS.**

The Style Bible changes rhetorical form, not business doctrine.

No canonical Layer 1 file was changed.

---

# Remaining risk

The Style Bible cannot prove its quality in isolation.

Its real test is the future golden-chapter prototype.

Therefore:

- Phase 3 is approved for use;
- its rules remain revisable if golden chapters reveal new systematic problems;
- full production remains blocked until Phase 6 approval.

---

# Final decision

**PHASE 3 = DONE / PASS.**

Next phase: define chapter grammar and pedagogical features without turning them into another rigid generation template.
