# Gate 1 — Functional grammar, not visible template

**PASS.**

The chapter system is expressed as five instructional functions:

1. orient;
2. model;
3. demonstrate;
4. apply;
5. integrate.

The functions do not require fixed headings or identical sequencing.

Three dominant chapter modes are available:

- conceptual/diagnostic;
- quantitative;
- procedural/operational.

This avoids replacing V1's rhetorical template with a new textbook-shaped template.

---

# Gate 2 — Beginner progression is explicit

**PASS.**

The system requires:

- concrete problem before unnecessary abstraction;
- minimal pre-training of terms;
- fully worked demonstration for difficult novice material;
- partially worked application;
- transfer to new contexts;
- guidance fading as competence increases.

This is coherent with the project's beginner-first contract and worked-example research.

---

# Gate 3 — Examples have differentiated jobs

**PASS.**

The system distinguishes:

- micro-example;
- worked example;
- counterexample;
- recurring case;
- real documented case;
- transfer case.

The distinction prevents V2 from treating any short hypothetical as sufficient pedagogical evidence.

---

# Gate 4 — Visuals are part of explanation

**PASS.**

Figures are planned inside the chapter argument through:

**question → figure → interpretation.**

The grammar also defines proper jobs for:

- tables;
- diagrams;
- charts;
- formulas;
- timelines;
- matrices;
- state maps.

The visual system itself remains a Phase 5 task.

---

# Gate 5 — Quantitative teaching has a complete sequence

**PASS.**

New metrics/formulas must be taught through:

business problem → quantities → formula/model → variable definition → worked calculation → interpretation → sensitivity → misuse/limit → transfer.

This is materially stronger than V1's definition-heavy treatment.

---

# Gate 6 — Retrieval and transfer are separated

**PASS.**

The system distinguishes:

- recall;
- interpretation;
- diagnosis;
- calculation;
- transfer;
- own-business application.

Part-level capstones require multi-chapter integration.

The manual therefore targets decision competence, not merely recognition of terms.

---

# Gate 7 — Feature palette is controlled

**PASS.**

Primary box taxonomy is limited to:

- PRINCIPIO
- ESEMPIO SVOLTO
- ERRORE FREQUENTE
- APPROFONDIMENTO
- STRUMENTO OPERATIVO
- CASO

Learning prompts are also limited to a small reusable family.

No feature is mandatory by quota.

---

# Gate 8 — Core narrative remains primary

**PASS.**

The chapter must remain understandable without optional deep-dive boxes.

Pedagogical features supplement or externalize specific learning functions; they do not compensate for fragmented prose.

---

# Remaining risk

The system is still theoretical until the project defines:

- exact visual language;
- recurring case architecture;
- real-case sourcing rules;
- quantitative/formula catalog;
- linked numerical datasets.

Those belong to Phase 5 and must be resolved before golden chapters.

---

# Final decision

**PHASE 4 = DONE / PASS.**

Next phase: design the visual, case and quantitative systems that golden chapters will actually use.
